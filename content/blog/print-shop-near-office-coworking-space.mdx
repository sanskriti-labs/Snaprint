Needing a print at work usually happens at the worst time — ten minutes before a meeting, right before you have to courier a signed document, or when the office printer is jammed again. Walking to the nearest print shop, waiting for it to be free, and walking back can eat half an hour you didn't have. Here's a faster way to think about it.

## Why office printing is more broken than it looks

Most offices and coworking spaces either don't have a printer at all, share one printer across dozens of desks, or have one that's perpetually out of toner. Even when it works, it's usually not set up for things like binding, color printing at volume, or printing from a phone when your laptop isn't with you. The result: people default to walking to the nearest xerox shop, which has its own friction — a queue, a wait for the shop to notice you, cash-only pricing, and shop hours that don't match office hours.

## What a self-service kiosk changes

A kiosk like Snaprint sits inside a shop near office clusters and coworking spaces and lets you skip the parts of that process that actually waste time:

1. **Scan the QR code on the kiosk.** No app to install, works on Android and iOS.
2. **Upload straight from your phone.** PDF, Word doc, or image — no need to email yourself a file or find a laptop.
3. **Pay via UPI.** GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, or any UPI app — no cash, no waiting for change.
4. **Collect your print**, usually in under a minute for a standard document.

There's no counter interaction required — you're not waiting for a staff member to be free, and you're not explaining what you need out loud while three other people are also trying to get a print done before a call.

## Where this actually helps

- **Before a meeting** — print an agenda, a handout, or a signed copy without leaving the building for twenty minutes.
- **Courier and delivery documents** — print, sign, and get something out the door without waiting on the one shared office printer.
