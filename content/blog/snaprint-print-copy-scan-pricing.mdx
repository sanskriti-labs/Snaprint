## How pricing works at a Snaprint kiosk

Every Snaprint kiosk sits inside an existing local shop — not a standalone booth with a head office setting prices from a spreadsheet. The shop owner who hosts the kiosk sets the per-page rate, the same way they always have, because they know their street, their footfall, and their regulars better than anyone else.

What Snaprint standardizes is everything *around* that price:

- A transparent rate displayed on the kiosk screen before you pay — no surprise charges after the print comes out.
- The same fast, self-service flow at every location: upload, confirm, pay, collect.
- UPI payment built in, so there's no fumbling for exact change or waiting for someone to find a ₹10 note.

That means exact per-page pricing will vary a little from one Snaprint shop to the next — a kiosk near a college in Bengaluru and one near a government office in a smaller town won't charge identically, just like two xerox shops on the same road rarely do. What stays constant is that the rate is visible, fixed at the point of payment, and never changes mid-transaction.
