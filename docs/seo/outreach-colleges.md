# College Outreach Prep  --  snaprints.com

A list of 10 Bengaluru colleges with strong print-demand fit (placement season, project reports, admit cards). The goal is to convert at least one placement officer into a free trial  --  every campus placement yields dozens of resume prints, plus a high-authority `.edu.in` backlink if we get listed on their internal placement-resources page.

---

## Pitch template (50 words)

Copy, customise the bracketed fields, and send via email or WhatsApp. Keep it under 60 words in the body.

```
Subject: Free print kiosk for [College] placement season

Hi [Name],

We built a self-service print kiosk that lets students print resumes,
project reports, and admit cards from their phone  --  no queue, no shop
visit. We placed one at [neighbouring college] last month.

Could we trial one at [College] for the placement season? Free for the
semester. ~30 seconds per print.

 --  Abhishek, Snaprint
```

**Follow-up cadence (if no reply):**

- Day 3: WhatsApp the placement cell's official number
- Day 7: Email the PR office (often the same person handles both)
- Day 14: Try the placement officer's personal LinkedIn (warm connect only  --  do not pitch in the connect note)

## Notes on the email column

The "Email guess" values are best-effort guesses based on standard Indian college domain patterns. **Always verify by:**

1. Visiting the official college website's contact page
2. Checking the placement cell's PDF brochure (often linked from their homepage)
3. Searching LinkedIn for "Placement Officer at [College]"

If a college's email bounces, look up the placement cell's **office landline** on the college website and call directly  --  getting past a receptionist is easier than you think for a free pilot offer.

---

## What success looks like

For each college that accepts a trial kiosk:

1. Sign a simple one-page MoU (template in `docs/legal/mou-template.md`  --  to be created when first needed).
2. Get listed on the college's placement-resources page (high-value `.edu.in` backlink).
3. Capture 1 testimonial email from the placement officer  --  quote it (with permission) on the snaprints.com homepage.
4. Add the college slug to `content/pseo/colleges.ts` so we publish a `/print-kiosk-[slug]` landing page indexed in Google.

Track status transitions in this file: ☐ contacted → ☐ reply received → ☐ pilot agreed → ☐ live.
