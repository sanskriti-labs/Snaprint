# Snaprint

Print in your hand in under 60 seconds. No shop attendant. No waiting in line.

Snaprint builds self service print kiosks for shops, campuses, and coworking spaces. A customer scans a QR code, sends a file from their phone, and walks away with a printout in under a minute. For shop owners, the kiosk keeps earning even after the counter closes.

Snaprint also runs a directory that helps people find a nearby xerox or print shop for black and white printing, colour printing, binding, and scanning, across Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Mumbai, Pune, and Delhi NCR.

## Who it's for

- Shop owners who want a second income stream from unused floor space
- Campuses and coworking spaces that want printing without hiring staff to run it
- Anyone who needs a print job done fast and can't find an open shop nearby
