# Snaprint

Print in your hand in under 60 seconds. No app, no queue, no shop attendant.

Snaprint is India's self-service print kiosk network, built and supported by Sanskriti Labs in Bengaluru. It solves two different problems for two different people: a fast, unattended way for a customer to get a document printed, and a way for a shop owner or a new small operator to run a printing business without standing behind a counter all day.

## For customers: how a print works

1. Scan the QR code on the kiosk screen with any phone camera, no app to install
2. Upload the file from your phone or a cloud drive (PDF, DOCX, or JPG)
3. Choose paper size, number of copies, and colour or black and white
4. Pay on the kiosk by UPI, card, or cash, then confirm
5. Collect the print from the output tray, in hand in under 60 seconds

Snaprint also runs a directory to help people find a listed xerox or print shop nearby for black and white printing, colour printing, binding, and scanning, across Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Mumbai, Pune, and Delhi NCR.

## For operators: running a kiosk as a business

Snaprint is also a route into owning a small, largely unattended printing business. This fits two kinds of people:

- **Existing shop owners** who want their printer earning during the hours and orders their counter currently misses (walk-ins after closing, remote uploads from students or offices nearby)
- **Someone starting a new small venture**, with no existing shop, who wants to place a kiosk on its own, at a college gate, inside a coworking space, or in a small rented spot, and run that as the entire business

What's included in the kiosk (the S1 hardware bundle):

- A dedicated print and scan unit with 2,000-sheet capacity, a 15.6 inch touch display, and UPS backup, with the Snaprint OS pre-installed
- A mobile app and dashboard showing live revenue, print counts, and ink and paper status, so the kiosk is run from a phone, not from the counter
- On-site installation and training in Bengaluru, plus six months of software support included
- Shop branding kit and a listing on the Snaprint network map so customers can find the kiosk

Ownership terms: you buy the kiosk once and own it outright. There is no revenue share, no per-print commission, and no monthly or annual software fee. Operation is 24/7 and unattended once installed.

## Pricing

Snaprint has three kiosk models, priced one time at ₹84,999, ₹1,39,999, and ₹2,99,999 plus 18% GST, depending on capacity. All three include hardware, software, installation, training, and six months of support. A typical mid-traffic Bengaluru shop, printing around 50 pages a day at an average ticket of ₹15, recoups the ₹84,999 model in roughly 5 to 6 months of operating cash flow. Higher-traffic locations, such as a college gate doing several hundred prints a day, recoup faster.

## Works with the hardware you already have

The Snaprint self-service software connects to your existing printer, Canon, HP, Epson, Brother, or any brand, without a forced hardware change. If you're adding a kiosk to an existing xerox or stationery shop, your current printer keeps working exactly as it does today.

## Privacy and security

Every uploaded file is deleted immediately after it prints. Uploads are encrypted end to end and secured with AES-256, both in transit and at rest. No document is ever stored, and nothing is shared with a third party.

## Frequently asked questions

**How much does a Snaprint kiosk cost?**
One time, from ₹84,999 to ₹2,99,999 plus 18% GST depending on the model, covering hardware, software, installation, training, and six months of support. No annual or recurring software fee.

**Is there a contract or lock-in period?**
You own the hardware from day one. There is a one-page agreement covering software and brand usage, with standard exit terms and no multi-year lock-in or early-exit penalty.

**Which areas does Snaprint currently support for kiosk installation?**
Installation, on-site training, and support are currently Bengaluru-based. If you're outside Bengaluru and interested, get in touch and expansion is discussed based on operator density in your area.

**Can I add a kiosk to my existing xerox or stationery shop?**
Yes. That's the most common setup: the kiosk sits inside an existing shop and captures the remote and after-hours orders the counter currently misses.

**Can I start a Snaprint kiosk as a new business, without an existing shop?**
Yes. A kiosk can be placed on its own, in a college gate, a coworking space, or a small rented spot, and run as a standalone small venture rather than an add-on to an existing shop.

**What is the realistic return on investment?**
At around 50 prints a day and a ₹15 average ticket, the ₹84,999 model typically recoups in 5 to 6 months. Actual returns depend on footfall, location, and average ticket size.

**What happens to a customer's file after printing?**
It is deleted immediately after the print completes. Nothing is stored or shared.
